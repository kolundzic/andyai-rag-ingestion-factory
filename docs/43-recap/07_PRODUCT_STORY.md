# Product Story

## The Problem

Organizations have too many documents and too little trustworthy access to what those documents contain.

## The Mistake

Many systems start with a chat box.

## Our Approach

AndyAI Knowledge Factory starts with document control.

## The Flow

```text
Upload documents.
Ingest pages.
Preserve metadata.
Create chunks.
Retrieve with permissions.
Package evidence.
Build Context Boards.
Draft with citations.
Approve outputs.
Observe and govern releases.
```

## The Promise

```text
Knowledge becomes usable without losing evidence.
```
