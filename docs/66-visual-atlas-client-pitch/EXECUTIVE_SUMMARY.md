# Executive Summary

AndyAI Knowledge Factory helps document-heavy organizations turn large PDF and document collections into structured, searchable, evidence-backed, human-approved knowledge systems.

## Problem

Organizations often have hundreds of thousands of pages trapped inside PDFs, reports, manuals, policies, research documents, contracts, or internal archives.

Ordinary search is shallow.
Ordinary RAG can retrieve fragments.
Serious organizations need traceability, approval, access control, and reusable knowledge.

## Solution

```text
documents
→ ingestion
→ retrieval
→ evidence packs
→ LLM Wiki
→ Knowledge Graph
→ human approval
→ visual atlas
→ exports and workflows
```

## Positioning

```text
Not a chatbot over files.
A governed knowledge production system.
```
