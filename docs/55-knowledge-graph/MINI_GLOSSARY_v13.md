# Mini Glossary v13

## RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation. In v13, RAG remains important, but it is not the entire architecture.

## LLM Wiki

A persistent AI-supported knowledge base where topics, entities, claims, and sources become linked wiki pages.

## Knowledge Graph

A graph where concepts, documents, claims, sources, and evidence are represented as nodes and edges.

## Hierarchical Knowledge Graph

A graph organized by levels: domain, topic, subtopic, entity, claim, evidence, source.

## Claim

A single reviewable statement extracted from documents or synthesis.

## Evidence Pack

A package of source references, citations, confidence, and audit trail supporting a claim or answer.

## Human Approval

A control layer where operators accept, reject, correct, or lock knowledge.

## Visual Atlas

A visual layer that lets users see knowledge as maps, flows, hierarchies, and evidence paths.
